### What to bring

- Choose the right tool: model-driven apps for data-heavy processes on Dataverse, canvas apps for
  task-focused or mobile experiences, Power Automate for workflow, Power BI for reporting.
- Watch for delegation limits in canvas apps, premium connector licensing, API request limits
  and flow run limits.
- Plan environments (dev, test, prod), solutions, and data loss prevention policies up front.
- Recommend Managed Environments and the CoE Starter Kit where governance matters.
